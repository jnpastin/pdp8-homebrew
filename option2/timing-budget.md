# Timing Budget: Two Ranks, Wide Write TP (2026-11-22)

Proposal, with `c5-timing-draft.patch`. It supersedes `timing.md` sections 3 to 5, and moves into
`docs/09-timing` in phase 10.

Generated by `python -I -X utf8 option2/experiments/timing_budget.py` from the v2 artifacts. Every
micro-operation of every case, every encoded control field and every control-store address input is
modeled; anything unknown to the model is an error. Times are ns from the TS boundary, at datasheet
worst case.

## What it assumes

- Lengths: fast 5/8/6/5, slow 6/8/7/7, console 9; a two-step TP in DEFER (auto-index) TS4, EXECUTE
  TS3 and TS4, INTERRUPT TS3, DMA TS2, and TPC
- Two ranks with buffered clocks, decision 11a, and a buffered control word
- DB strobes from `IOT_TRANSFER` at 100 ns (applied); `/INITIALIZE` decoded and `/RD` and the MDB drive
  qualified off during IOTs (correction 2, applied); `/INT_REQ` qualified by the SRQ decode in the overlay
- 5 ns for each crossing, 50 pF on each output, 200 pF and 220 ohms on open-collector lines
- ACT or F parts throughout; main memory no slower than an MR256A08B
- STOP, the mode switches and FP_SR captured at a TP, and `/INT_REQ` not resynchronized (C2, applied);
  no `/IO_WAIT`

## What it does not cover

- Ringing, crosstalk and ground bounce
- The panel input register's settling, and the console-command synchronizers
- The shadow copy and power-on sequence
- Front-panel display paths
- The two priority encoders are allowances (45 and 20 ns), not datasheet figures
- The address-translator and write-gate delays use the 3.3 V LVC parts' figures
